The longer it waits

Every wet winter turns it into a bigger job

Water that gets through a flat roof doesn't stop at the felt. It soaks the deck boards, then the joists underneath, and by the time it shows on your ceiling the timber has often been wet for months. What could have been a straightforward re-roof becomes new joists, new plaster, and a much bigger bill. And slow leaks are something home insurance often won't cover.

None of that is inevitable. It just needs a roof that's built properly the first time.

The fix

Roofing done properly, from the joists up

We don't lay new felt over old problems. Every job starts by finding out what's really going on underneath and fixing that, so the roof you pay for is the last one you'll need for decades.

What you're paying for

What's actually under a Roofit roof

Most flat roofs fail because of what's underneath, not what's on top. Here's one of our garage roofs, from rotten to watertight.

  1. 01

    Strip back to the joists

    The old felt and decking come off so we can check every joist and replace anything that's rotten.

  2. 02

    New 18mm deck

    Tongue-and-groove boards give a solid, flat base with no soft spots and no dips for water to sit in.

  3. 03

    Edge trims fitted

    Profiled GRP trims on every edge for crisp, clean lines and water that runs straight into the gutter.

  4. 04

    Fibreglass laminated

    Resin reinforced with a heavier glass matt than the usual 450g standard. Laid cold, in one piece, with no joints.

  5. 05

    Topcoat & finish

    A tough protective topcoat in the colour of your choice. Maintenance-free from day one.
